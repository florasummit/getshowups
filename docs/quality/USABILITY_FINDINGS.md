# Usability findings ledger (evaluation of 2026-11-25)

Source: a zero-instruction evaluation by six independent browser agents on a local copy of the product with fictional
businesses (browser only; no code, tests or docs read). 85 observed findings. Severity: S1 task could not be completed ·
S2 completed after an error, a wrong turn or a guess · S3 slowed down or unsure · S4 polish. Every finding ends as
**resolved** (with the test that guards it), **retained** (with the reason) or **blocked** (with the dependency).
The AI model was a scripted stand-in during the evaluation (DASH-01, TEACH-01, TEACH-03 are partly coloured by that).
