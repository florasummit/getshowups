# Customer zero: PoolThru (founder-assisted, unbilled)

## Seen working in production, 2026-11-24 (read-only)
Booking page with 160 open times · chat page on, voice available · website served · calendar readable · account active.

## Can be offered today
Hosted website and booking page · bookings into Google Calendar with Google's own invitation · service-area screening ·
AI receptionist on the website (chat, browser voice) · dashboard, outcomes, payments recorded by hand · follow-up inbox (read) ·
dashboard assistant (AI wording is limited until the model decision; records always show).

## Not available: don't promise
Email or text to customers (test recipients only) · phone numbers and forwarding · live ads and ad management · card payments ·
Teach Your AI affecting live answers · self-service sign-up · guaranteed results of any kind.

## Before starting
- [ ] Owner of PoolThru confirms: hours, service ZIPs, services, prices, calendar, what customers should know
- [ ] Written pilot understanding (attorney reviews wording): unbilled, what is tested, how data is used and deleted, how to stop
- [ ] Google "unverified app" screen explained, or verification finished
- [ ] Founder alerts confirmed in the inbox

## Day 1 (about 45 minutes together)
1. Sign in, check the calendar account. 2. Read every setting aloud once. 3. Book and cancel one test visit; mark it as test.
4. Try the chat with three real customer questions. 5. Put the booking link on the website, Google profile and voicemail text.
6. Show: assistant, marking outcomes, recording payments.

## Weekly, 15 minutes
Mark every visit · record every payment · answer every inquiry · read Business Review incidents.

## What we measure (from records; compare with the owner's own memory of the month before, written down on day 1)
| Outcome | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Faster response | time from inquiry to first message or booking |
| More qualified bookings | in-area inquiries and share booked |
| Fewer no-shows | share of marked visits that happened |
| Less admin | owner's own estimate of minutes a week, day 1 and day 30 |
| Money | payments recorded; never credited to ads |
Say "too few to judge" below 10 inquiries. Review on day 30; decide: continue, change, or stop.
